Free query-list builder

Paste bank lines (one per line, from Xero, QuickBooks, FreeAgent, Sage or a CSV export) and get a triage of every line, a grouped and numbered client message, a WhatsApp version for short lists, notes for the accountant and a table to work from. Everything runs in your browser: nothing you paste is sent anywhere, and account or card numbers are cut to the last 4 digits.


The builder reads descriptions only, using common UK and US bank and supplier patterns, so it suggests rather than decides. It never books money in as sales without asking. Check every Low-confidence line and every accountant note before you post anything.
