What it does with a messy suspense account

You paste five unexplained lines from a mixed-use sole-trader account (not VAT registered):

DateDescriptionAmountProposed
03/09FPS J SMITH−1,200.00ask wages, subcontractor, loan, personal? accountant payroll/CIS
05/09AMZN MKTP UK*2K4−86.40confirm supplies (medium)
05/09TESCO STORES 3301−42.17confirm personal (likely)
08/09TFR FROM M JONES+2,000.00ask sale, loan or capital? (never assumed to be sales)
12/09ADOBE *CREATIVE CLD−56.98high software

↓ and drafts this for the client (plus a WhatsApp version)

To the client

Hi Sam, a few quick ones so your accounts are right and you don't pay more tax than you need to. Reply with the number and your answer, e.g. "1 subcontractor". By Friday 30th, please.

  1. 3 Sept, £1,200 to J Smith. Wages / subcontractor / loan repayment / personal / other?
  2. 8 Sept, £2,000 in from M Jones. A sale / a loan / your own money / other?
  3. 5 Sept, Amazon £86.40. We think this is supplies. OK, or what was it?
  4. 5 Sept, Tesco £42.17. We think this is personal. OK, or business?

For you

  1. Adobe: posted to Software, no question needed.
  2. J Smith: if it's labour, check payroll status. If it's construction, CIS applies.
  3. M Jones: hold in suspense until the client answers. Not sales by default.
  4. Check: 5 lines. Out £1,385.55, in £2,000.00, matching your input.

Every figure comes from what you pasted. Lines it can't place are left as questions.

client-explainer

The answer in the first sentence, then the client's own numbers laid out so they add up. Download the guide

2. Make your assistant in the app you use.

  1. In ChatGPT, click GPTs in the sidebar, then + Create.
  2. Open the Configure tab. Name it Bookkeeping Assistant.
  3. Paste the instructions into Instructions (Ctrl+V, or ⌘V on a Mac).
  4. Under Knowledge, click Upload files and add bookkeeping-assistant-guide.md.
  5. Click Create, choose Only me, and save. It's in your sidebar now.

Questions

Is it safe to put client data into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude?

Keep it to what the task needs. Use client codes or first names, use only the last 4 digits of account numbers, and never paste NI or Social Security numbers, UTRs, passwords or ID documents. Check your practice's data-protection policy and your AI app's data settings, or use a business plan that doesn't train on your data.

Does it give tax advice?

No. It drafts client messages, query lists and checklists. Tax rules, thresholds and deadlines are included as prompts to check against gov.uk, irs.gov or your professional body, and judgement calls such as capital vs revenue or director's loans are flagged for the accountant.

Does it work with Xero, QuickBooks, FreeAgent or Sage?

It works alongside them. Paste or upload exports such as uncategorised bank lines, a trial balance or aged debtors, and it returns tables you can work from and messages you can send through your portal or email. It doesn't connect to your software or post anything.

My side-hustle clients keep their sales records in a mess. Anything for them?

Yes: point them to our bookkeeping spreadsheet for Vinted, eBay, Etsy and Depop sellers (£12.99, Excel or Google Sheets). It works out platform fees and gives tax-year totals you can work from, and the free sellertaxcalculator.co.uk gives them a tax estimate.

A client is shocked by their January bill. How do I explain payments on account?

The free client-explainer skill writes it in plain English with their own figures (the bill, the first payment on account, then July). To show them the numbers first, send them our free payments on account calculator: it covers the £1,000 and 80% rules, the 31 January and 31 July amounts, and reducing payments with SA303.

A client can't pay their bill by 31 January. What do I tell them?

File on time anyway, then set up a payment plan (Time to Pay): online for bills up to £30,000, once the return is filed. Agreed before the 30-day penalty date and kept to, it stops the 5% late payment penalties, though interest still runs. Our free can't-pay guide compares paying late with a plan, and the client-explainer skill turns it into a message with their figures.

Clients keep asking about Making Tax Digital. Is there something I can send them?

Yes. Our free MTD for Income Tax guide covers the thresholds, the 7th-of-the-month deadlines and the first-year penalty rules (no points in 2026/27, or in 2027/28 for April 2027 joiners), with a checker for each client's start date and a client email you can adapt. The free client-explainer skill then writes a version with that client's own figures.

Does the Pro onboarding skill replace my AML policy?

No. It's a working checklist based on the Money Laundering Regulations 2017 and HMRC's published guidance. Your practice's risk assessment, your policies and your supervisor's guidance come first, and your nominated officer makes the risk decisions.
